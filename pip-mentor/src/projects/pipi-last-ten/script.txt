Pipi has ten dollars left. Rent is due tomorrow.
Then a guru texts him. Put it all in. Guaranteed.
Pipi's thumb hovers over the button.
Then he remembers what grandma always said. Never bet the rent.
So he risks one dollar. Just one.
The trade loses. Pipi still has nine.
The guru? His Lamborghini was rented. The repo man took it by lunch.
Next week, Pipi wins three dollars. He buys grandma a tea.
Day one. Would you have pressed the button?
