# Pipi series - episode log

Keep this file updated after every post. The videos themselves can be deleted: the scripts (`dialogue.txt` / `script.txt`), drawing code (`Film.tsx`) and voices are all in `pip-mentor/src/projects/<slug>/`, so any episode can be re-rendered from the Actions tab (Render film -> slug).

## Cast and voices
- **Pipi** - mint hoodie, tuft of hair, broke (patch on the hoodie). Voice: en-US-EricNeural.
- **The Guru** - gold suit, glasses, fake wealth (his Lamborghini was rented). Voice: en-US-ChristopherNeural. Disguise: trench coat, fedora, fake mustache (ep 3).
- **Grandma** - pink cardigan, bun, glasses, wooden spoon ("certified in spoons"). Voice: en-GB-SoniaNeural. Her rule: never bet the rent.
- **Repo Man** - orange vest, remote, magnet (ep 1).
- **The Devil** (red, horns, pitchfork; voice en-US-RogerNeural) and **The Angel** (white robe, halo; voice en-US-JennyNeural) - tiny, sit on Pipi's shoulders (ep 4).
- Ep 1 and the roast used a narrator (en-US-GuyNeural). From ep 2 on: no narrator, the characters talk.

## Running gags / continuity
- Pipi's money: starts with $10 (ep 1), risks $1, loses it, has $9, wins $3, buys grandma a tea -> back to $9. Ep 2 and 3 start with "nine dollars". Ep 4: he loses another $1 -> $8. Ep 5: wins $2 -> $10.
- Moth flying out of his empty pocket (ep 1). Grandma's spoon. The guru's rented Lamborghini. Every ep ends on "Episode N ..." plus a stamp.
- Teaching beats: never bet the rent / risk 1% (ep 1), gurus sell courses not profits (ep 2), no proof, no pay: ask for the losses (ep 3).
