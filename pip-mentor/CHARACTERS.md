# pip.mentor cast (drawn in code, identical in every film)

**Pipi** - the hero. Round white head, one tuft of hair, mint hoodie (#2fbf8f), white sneakers. Warm, a bit dramatic, learns the hard way.
- Poor Pipi: patch on the hoodie, empty pockets when needed. Rich Pipi: gold chain + round glasses. Same face, same hoodie.
- He can be broke in one story and rich in the next. He is always the one the viewer roots for.

Supporting cast (reusable): **Grandma** (pink cardigan, bun, glasses, wooden spoon, "never bet the rent"), **The Guru** (gold suit, glasses, rented Lamborghini), **Repo Man** (orange vest, remote), **The Regulator** (grey coat, clipboard), **The Broker** (gold tie, sly).

Formats: roast, education, story, wealth, advice, jokes. Every film opens with a scroll-stopping hook in the first 2 seconds.
Sizes: 16:9 (YouTube) and 9:16 (Reels / Shorts / TikTok). Narration: free Microsoft voice (en-US-GuyNeural).
